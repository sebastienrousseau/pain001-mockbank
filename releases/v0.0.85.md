# pain001-mockbank v0.0.85

## Highlights ⭐️

* **Archive collected replies**: a new `/archive` mailbox (`--archive`) lets a client move a reply out of `/outbox` once it has it, as `pain001 download --after-fetch move:/archive` does. No other rename across mailboxes is allowed, and nothing is overwritten.
* **pain.008 direct debits answered**: uploads of pain.008 get a pain.002 reply under the same rules as pain.001 credit transfers, naming pain.008 as the original message.
* **In lockstep with pain001 0.0.85**: the mock bank can now stand in for a bank in end-to-end tests of the core's new `pain001 download` and `pain001 status` commands.

## Details

`make_reply` was split into parsing, amount and row helpers, which takes it under the complexity ceilings and out of the baseline. pyproject.toml, `__version__` and the README's image tag move to 0.0.85 together.
